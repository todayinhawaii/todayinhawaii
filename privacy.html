Privacy Policy

We believe in full transparency about how we handle your data. Short version: we collect very little and never sell anything.

✅ Last updated: May 2026

🌺 The simple version: Today in Hawaii (todayinhawaii.com) is a free travel information resource. We don't sell your data, we don't require you to create an account, and we don't store any personal information about you on our servers.

1. Who We Are

Today in Hawaii (todayinhawaii.com) is an independent Hawaii travel information website providing live weather, surf reports, tourist statistics, hotel and restaurant recommendations, and travel booking tools for visitors to the Hawaiian Islands.

We are not affiliated with the State of Hawaii, DBEDT, HTA, or any government agency.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use this website. All content is freely accessible without registration.

Information collected automatically

When you visit todayinhawaii.com, standard web technologies may automatically collect:

  • Pages you visit and time spent on each page
  • General geographic location (country/region level only)
  • Device type, browser type and operating system
  • Referring website (how you found us)

This information is collected in aggregate and cannot be used to identify you personally.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This helps us improve the content and features we offer. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous usage data.

Google Analytics does NOT collect your name, email address or any personally identifiable information. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies:

  • Google Analytics cookies — anonymous usage tracking (can be disabled)
  • Session cookies — temporary, deleted when you close your browser

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or any third-party marketing cookies.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website integrates the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Windy.com — live weather radar embed
  • Open-Meteo — weather data API (no personal data shared)
  • Google Maps — interactive island maps
  • Expedia / Rentalcars.com — travel search (you are directed to their sites to book)
  • Surfline — surf camera links
  • timeanddate.com — sunrise/sunset calendar links

When you click through to any of these services, their own privacy policies apply. We recommend reviewing them before making any bookings.

6. Travel Booking

Our travel search widget directs you to third-party booking platforms (Expedia, Rentalcars.com etc). We do not process, store or have access to any payment information, passport details or booking data. All transactions occur entirely on those third-party platforms.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes. Full stop.

8. Children's Privacy

Today in Hawaii is a general-audience travel information website. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data including:

  • The right to know what data we hold about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of analytics tracking

Since we collect very little personal data, these rights are straightforward to exercise. Contact us at the email below.

10. Security

Our website is served over HTTPS (secure, encrypted connection). We take reasonable precautions to protect the limited data we do collect, though no internet transmission is 100% secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
