Papakolea Green Sand Beach
📍 Ka'u · South Point
🤿 Snorkel⭐
👨‍👩‍👧 Family⭐⭐

One of only four green sand beaches in the entire world. The stunning green color comes from olivine crystals eroded from the surrounding volcanic cone. Getting here requires a 5.5 mile round trip hike or a bumpy 4WD truck ride.

💡 Pro Tip: Walk or hire a local 4WD truck ($20). Do not swim — extremely dangerous currents. Best green color in afternoon sunlight.
⏰ Best Time: Afternoon for best green color in sunlight
🅿️ Parking: Park at South Point Road end — hike or truck from there

📍 North Kohala · End of Road
🤿 Snorkel⭐
👨‍👩‍👧 Family⭐⭐

A remote and dramatic black sand beach at the bottom of the Pololu Valley Trail. Wild, untouched and hauntingly beautiful with massive sea cliffs on both sides. No swimming — violent currents. Pure raw Hawaii.
