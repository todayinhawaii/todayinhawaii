Today in Hawaii was built with one simple goal — to be the most useful, most honest and most beautiful Hawaii travel resource on the internet. No fluff, no fake reviews, no paywalls. Just real data, real recommendations and real aloha spirit.

Live Tourist Counts

Real daily visitor statistics for all 4 Hawaiian islands, based on official DBEDT flight arrival data. Updated every morning so you know exactly how busy Hawaii is today.

Live Weather Radar

Real-time animated weather radar powered by Windy.com with 46 specific location buttons — check Wailea, Kapalua, Paia, North Shore and everywhere in between.

Live Surf Report

Real-time wave heights and conditions for all four islands from the Open-Meteo Marine API. Know before you go whether it's pumping, good or flat.

Sunrise & Sunset Times

Exact sunrise and sunset times for every Hawaiian island — today and the next 7 days. Plan your sunrise hike up Haleakalā or your sunset dinner in Lahaina perfectly.
